Things to do

There’s plenty to do on the farm, and when you want to go out, Route 151 is right there — breakfast, skiing, hiking, beer, cider.

On the farm

The hot tub is set up for a crowd after a cold day. There’s a bocce court, a fire pit, a trampoline and swings, and a few spots to eat outside. Highland cattle and mini donkeys graze the pastures (please don’t go into fields they’re using). The pond and the South Rockfish River are good for a swim or a fishing line when it’s warm.

  • Private hot tub
  • Bocce, fire pit, and outdoor tables
  • Trampoline, slackline, and tree swings
  • Pond and river
  • Games in the barn and icehouse
  • Walk over to Blue Toad Hard Cider

Breakfast: Belties

Crowd-pleaser for coffee and breakfast, a short drive from the farm. Good first stop before a hike or a day on the mountain.

belties.com

Nelson Farmers Market

Saturdays, May through October. Produce, baked goods, and other local stuff — a good stop if you’re cooking at the house that night.

nelsonfarmersmarketcooperative.com

Wintergreen

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, hiking and long views the rest of the year. The resort is just up the mountain from us.

wintergreenresort.com

Hikes

Wintergreen has a big trail system. Closer to the valley, the Rockfish Valley trails are an easy walk. For a destination hike, Crabtree Falls and Humpback Rocks (Blue Ridge Parkway) are both nearby.

  • Wintergreen trails
  • Rockfish Valley trails
  • Crabtree Falls
  • Humpback Rocks

Beer and cider

Devil’s Backbone Basecamp is here in Roseland. Three Notch’d and Bold Rock are a few minutes up 151 in Nellysford. Blue Toad is on Winery Lane — you can walk. Blue Mountain Brewery in Afton is an easy extra stop.

  • Devil’s Backbone
  • Three Notch’d (151)
  • Bold Rock
  • Blue Toad Hard Cider
  • Blue Mountain Brewery

Blue Ridge Mountain Maze

Corn maze and fall festival at Blue Toad, basically next door. Weekends from September into early November — pumpkins, night maze, cider donuts, the usual October chaos, in a good way.

blueridgemountainmaze.com
