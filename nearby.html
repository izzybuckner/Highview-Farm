Around High View

Things to do

Days on the farm, and a short drive along Route 151 for breakfast, skiing, hiking, beer, and cider.

On the farm

Hot tub, bocce, fire pit, and outdoor tables. Highland cattle and mini donkeys graze the pastures — please stay out of fields they’re using. Pond and river for swimming and fishing.

  • Private hot tub
  • Bocce, fire pit, and outdoor tables
  • Trampoline, slackline, and tree swings
  • Pond and river
  • Games in the barn and icehouse
  • Walk over to Blue Toad Hard Cider

Breakfast: Belties

Coffee, biscuits, and a favorite breakfast a short drive from the farm — a good first stop before a hike or a day on the mountain.

belties.com

Nelson Farmers Market

Saturdays, May through October. Produce, bread, and whatever is in season — easy to bring home for dinner at the house.

nelsonfarmersmarketcooperative.com

Wintergreen

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, hiking and long views the rest of the year. The resort is just up the mountain from us.

wintergreenresort.com

Hikes

Wintergreen has a big trail system. Closer to the valley, the Rockfish Valley trails are an easy walk. For a destination hike, Crabtree Falls and Humpback Rocks (Blue Ridge Parkway) are both nearby.

  • Wintergreen trails
  • Rockfish Valley trails
  • Crabtree Falls
  • Humpback Rocks

Beer and cider

Devil’s Backbone Basecamp is here in Roseland. Three Notch’d and Bold Rock are a few minutes up 151 in Nellysford. Blue Toad is on Winery Lane — you can walk. Blue Mountain Brewery in Afton is an easy extra stop.

  • Devil’s Backbone
  • Three Notch’d (151)
  • Bold Rock
  • Blue Toad Hard Cider
  • Blue Mountain Brewery

Blue Ridge Mountain Maze

Corn maze and fall festival at Blue Toad, next door. Weekends from September into early November — pumpkins, a night maze, and cider donuts.

blueridgemountainmaze.com
